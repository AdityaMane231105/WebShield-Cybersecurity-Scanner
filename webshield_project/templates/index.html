Who We Are

About WebShield

Revolutionizing Online Security with Advanced AI Detection

WebShield makes phishing protection easy to understand and fast to use. Our platform transforms URL risk analysis into clear, action-oriented guidance so users can browse with confidence.

Our Mission

Protect people from phishing by delivering accurate URL risk detection with simple, transparent explanations. We believe cybersecurity tools should teach as much as they defend.

How WebShield Works

We inspect each URL across multiple threat signals: domain patterns, HTTPS validation, IP-based links, suspicious keywords, hidden subdomains, and complex query strings. The results are combined into a single 0–100 risk score with LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH threat levels.

Why WebShield?

Instead of leaving you guessing, WebShield explains why a link is risky. This helps students, researchers, and everyday users recognize phishing tactics and make safer decisions online.
